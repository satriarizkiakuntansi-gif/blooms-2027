SOP Blooms 2027

Tap each section to read the rules. Please read everything before the event starts.

1Arrival & Check-In
  • Arrive at the venue at least 30 minutes before the event starts.
  • Do your Garden Check-In in this app and upload a clear selfie.
  • One check-in per participant. Ask the committee if you have trouble.
2Dress Code
  • Wear neat and polite attire.
  • Bright spring colors are welcome. Let's make the garden bloom!
  • Bring your participant ID or student card.
3Conduct & Respect
  • Respect the committee, speakers, and fellow participants.
  • Keep your phone on silent during sessions.
  • Keep the garden clean. Throw your trash in the bins.
4Photobooth Queue
  • Join the queue from the Booth Queue menu and get your number.
  • Come to the booth when you are 2 spots away.
  • If you miss your turn, the committee may skip you to the next person.
5Group Activities
  • Stay with your group during group sessions.
  • Check Daftar Kelompok to see who is in your group.
  • Follow the instructions of your group mentor.
6Safety & Belongings
  • Keep your valuables with you at all times.
  • Report any problem or emergency to the nearest committee member.
  • Follow the evacuation instructions if announced.
🌼 Thank you for following the rules and growing together!
